# Salamander Grand Piano samples

These MP3 samples are a compact subset of Alexander Holm's Salamander Grand
Piano, distributed by the Tone.js audio repository. The original instrument is
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

- Author: Alexander Holm
- Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
- Distribution: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The app loads samples spaced in minor thirds and pitch-shifts the nearest sample
by no more than two semitones to cover F-sharp1 through C6.
